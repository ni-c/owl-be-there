# Owl Be There

**Find the day that works for everyone.**

Owl Be There helps a group pick a day — for a match, a tournament, a hike or a barbecue. The organiser picks the candidate days, everyone marks the days they can make it on a calendar, and the calendar turns into a heatmap that shows at a glance when the whole group is free.

It is a cross between [Rallly](https://github.com/lukevella/rallly) (whole-day polls without accounts) and [Crab Fit](https://github.com/GRA0007/crab.fit) (paint your availability, see the group's heatmap) — with whole days on a calendar instead of lists or time slots.

> **Status:** under construction. The first release is on its way.

## Planned for the first release

- **Mark days the way you think about them:** tap a day to switch it on or off, drag across the calendar to mark a whole block — say, every weekend in March — in one go.
- **Yes and maybe:** a second brush for "if need be".
- **The group at a glance:** a heatmap calendar, the best days ranked, and who can make it on each day.
- **Multi-day events:** looking for a whole weekend? Ask for two days in a row and get the best blocks.
- **No accounts:** your name, and a password only if you want one. The organiser gets a private admin link.
- **Minimal data:** no e-mail addresses, no IP addresses, no cookies, no tracking. Events delete themselves 90 days after the last change.
- **Made for phones:** a calendar that fits the screen, big tap targets, share via the system share sheet or a QR code.
- **Self-hostable:** one container, one SQLite file.
